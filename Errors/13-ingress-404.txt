##########
Without Ingress, you may create a separate LoadBalancer Service for every application:

Internet
   ├── Load Balancer 1 → frontend-service
   ├── Load Balancer 2 → catalogue-service
   └── Load Balancer 3 → user-service

With Ingress, one load balancer can route requests to multiple Services:

                         ┌── /          → frontend-service
Internet → Load Balancer ├── /catalogue → catalogue-service
                         └── /user      → user-service

Ingress supports HTTP/HTTPS routing based mainly on hostname and URL path.
