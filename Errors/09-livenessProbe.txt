livenessProbe: check health of api inside conatiner if health is not good then it will restart the conatiner entire container lifecycle
[ ec2-user@ip-172-31-20-118 ~ ]$ kubectl get pods -n roboshop
NAME     READY   STATUS    RESTARTS   AGE
x        0/1     Running   4         144m

livenessProbe:
    httpsGet:
        path: /health
        port: 80
    intialDelaySeconds: 10
    periodSeconds: 5 

RESTARTS increases if liveinessprobe finds app is unhealthy 


causes:
    app startup may require long time
    wrong path or port
    probe timout is short


    A badly configured liveness probe can restart healthy containers, particularly during high load. 
    Startup probes can protect slow-starting applications until initialization finishes.

 ## Readiness failure: Stops traffic to the Pod.
    Liveness failure: Restarts the container.
